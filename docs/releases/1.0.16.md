# 1.0.16 — a photo is answered once

**Photos barely sent.** A device wanting a picture asks for its chunks
on every sync cycle — every two seconds while somebody is looking — and
the holder answered every copy of the ask it collected with a full
eight-megabyte answer into the same mailbox. The mailbox on the relay
has a quota; it filled within a cycle, every answer was cut short with
"quota exceeded", and the asking device drained eight megabytes of
mostly duplicate chunks per cycle. Read from the owner's Mac: two
devices asking for 58 and 29 chunks, held for half an hour.

Now the holder keeps a book: a chunk put into a mailbox is not put
there again for three minutes — long enough for a phone in the
background to collect it, short enough that a lost answer is repaired
by the next ask after the window. Each asking device gets its own copy.

**The Doorbell rows read as words**, not as `{parked}` and `{pong}`:
the diagnostics panel's templates were never filled in.

No relay change.
