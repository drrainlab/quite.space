# 1.0.21 — the light shell

The app looked complicated, and it was counted before anything was
touched: about twenty-five controls on screen before the first message, a
right-hand panel open by default, four header buttons each with a tilt, a
shadow, a breathing halo and a colour of its own, conversation set in
small mono type, and every message reserving room for reactions nobody had
left. Nothing in this release removes a capability. It decides what is
allowed to be **visible at rest**.

**The conversation.** Messages are set to be read — a sans face at a
comfortable size instead of terminal mono. Everyone has a face, a name and
a time on one line, and everyone — you included — sits in one column; your
own messages are still told apart by their tint. Reactions live under the
message and an empty row takes no room. Reply, forward, keep, react and
"should have noticed" are a small toolbar of marks that arrives beside the
message on hover or on a tap, and costs no height. A photo or a video that
stands alone is drawn without a bubble.

**The room's bar is one row**: the space's face, its name, how many people,
then every view as a pill — Chat, Materials, Shelf, Objects, Field, Posts —
and on the right "invite" and "⋯", which holds customize, the reaction
palette, the share link and the space's panel. **That panel is no longer a
column**: it slides over the conversation when asked for and leaves on
Escape, a click outside or ✕. If a space has instruments, their freshness
is readable from the bar without opening anything.

**The header** is the orbit, the connection light, *create* as the one
pill, *join*, *discover* and *signals* as plain words, settings, and your
own face. The connection speaks in words only when it has something other
than "healthy" to say. Behind the orbit a faint nebula arrives from above;
it drifts a few pixels over forty seconds and rests when nobody is looking.

**The composer is one rounded bar** — "+", the words, presence, the
microphone, send. **The sidebar** shows only the sections that have
something in them; pinning and grouping are where they always were, in
each row's menu.

**Colour.** Both themes are neutral now: the dark ground is the blue-black
of a night sky rather than aubergine, the light one is cool paper, and the
accent is a steel blue. A space's own tint is a *hue*; how light and how
saturated it is belongs to the theme, so every kind of space is equally
bright to the eye and "invite" is readable on white as well as at night.

**A voice of its own.** Anything the machine reports — a time, a count, a
section label — is small mono; anything a person wrote is set to be read.
Where a key does the thing, the key is drawn ("/" in search, "↵" by send),
on devices that have a keyboard. Dialogs are sheets: a larger radius, the
room blurred behind, filled fields, and choices as one grouped list. Under
the list of spaces a small plate says which terminal this is.

**A post opens as itself.** The two doors on every card — "Open" and "Open
with sound" — are gone. A post with an atmosphere opens with it, and with
its sound when it has one; Mute, Pause and Leave sit beside it while it
plays. *Settings → Appearance → Atmosphere sound* is two answers now,
*Plays* or *Never*. A temporary preview of a space you have not kept still
never starts sound on its own.

**Fixed on the way.** On the Field map, **"Place marker" did not place a
marker — it switched read receipts on** (and Cancel switched them off): two
controls had been wired to the wrong handlers. If your read-receipt setting
changed by itself after using the map, this is why; check it once in
Settings. The header's four actions no longer jump to the wrong side of
the settings button after the window crosses the phone width and back.

What the release deliberately does **not** draw, because the app does not
have it: message search, an inbox/starred/archive trio, a global "online"
dot. The reasoning is in `docs/plans/UI-2-LIGHT-SHELL.md`.

No relay change. No protocol change.

*There is no 1.0.20.* Its build stopped on a test whose fixture had simply
grown old — it was written on 17 August, a public projection rightly drops
ordinary messages after thirty days, and on 16 September the test's two
messages aged out of their own projection. Nothing was published under that
number, and a tag is never moved; this is the same release with the test
telling the truth again.
