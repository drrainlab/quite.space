# 1.0.12 — "I moved", and a guess that finds you

Two small things on top of 1.0.11, both about the first message after
something changed.

**"I moved."** When a device changes relays — automatic selection picked
a closer one, a network came and went, the app was closed on one network
and opened on another — it now tells every peer in every shared space
where it listens, in one frameless bundle, before its first cycle on the
new relay. Peers used to learn this only when the moved device next
wrote something; a device that only read stayed unreachable at its old
address until the route timed out.

**A guess that finds you.** A recipient nobody holds a route for, but
who wrote within the last month, is offered the message at every
official relay rather than only at the sender's — the delta book keeps
that to one copy of the history per relay and then only what is new. A
device with no sign of life in a month keeps the single guess it always
had: a retired phone must not cost the relays three histories.

Neither is a lookup; there is none, on purpose. They remove the need for
one on the two paths where the first message used to wait.
