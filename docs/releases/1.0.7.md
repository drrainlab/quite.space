# 1.0.7 — four skies

```
   ·  ✦   ·        nebula · aurora · caustics · silk        ·   ✦
 ✦    ·     ·      one floor under all of them              ·      ·
```

An atmosphere is the layer behind a post's words: a scene name, a seed,
four colours and three small numbers, signed into the post. This release
replaces the scenes a person can pick with four new ones, and every one
of them is a fragment shader — a tiny program that answers one question,
"what colour is this pixel at this moment", on your graphics chip.

**Why a program is allowed here.** A fragment shader has no memory, no
network and no eyes. It cannot read the post, the room or you. The only
thing it can do is be bright — and brightness is exactly what the floor
already governs: every frame is metered before it reaches the screen and
may move by at most one small step of luminance, the rule derived from
the general flash threshold. A sky can drift, breathe and glow. It cannot
flash, whatever it computes. [ADR-037](../../adr/ADR-037-a-scene-may-be-a-shader-never-a-program.md)
is the decision; the numbers are in it.

**The four.** *Nebula* — clouds of the post's own colours and a few seeded
stars. *Aurora* — curtains of light over a dark ground. *Caustics* — the net
of light on the floor of a pool. *Silk* — sheets of cloth folding in slow
air. The three knobs are the same everywhere: drift, density, glow. The
colours are the post's palette, so the poster you see before the sky
starts moving and the sky it becomes agree.

**The old six are retired, not removed.** The composer no longer offers
Drift, Dust, Ember, Orbit, Pulse or Tide — and nothing else changes. Every
post that already names one keeps its picture, on every device. A scene
id, once published, is a promise to every post that names it.

**A truer meter.** The readback that the floor rests on is taken in two
stages now: a single 90× downscale was picking pixels rather than
averaging them, and a caustic overshot the step law by 13 % on that
noise. All four scenes now measure within it.

**Where to look.** The official catalog's *Atmospheres* space carries one
post per scene with its recipe, and the four demo spaces each got a
post in their own sky — a night in the greenhouse, a bracket through
three machines, a mix that changed, a route read from the track. The
Studio one carries a quiet pad behind it, if your device allows sound.

**If you see only words.** A device without a graphics context, a reader
who turned atmospheres off, or a build older than this one shows a still
and the author's sentence instead. The picture may degrade. The meaning
may not.
