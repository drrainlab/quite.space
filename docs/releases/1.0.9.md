# 1.0.9 — bytes on their way, said so

The owner, reading a fresh post on another device: a picture slot with
the browser's broken-image glyph, a video slot as a black box with a dead
play button, and — a long while later — *media temporarily unavailable*.
The bytes were honestly on their way through the relay the whole time.
Nothing said so.

**A stand-in where the picture will be.** While a post's image, video,
audio or cover is being fetched, the element stays sourceless and takes
no room, and a quiet note stands in its place: *fetching from relay…*,
with the chunk count and the rail once a round has answered. The note
leaves the moment the fetch ends either way — the bytes arrive and the
media takes the note's place, or the unavailable sentence takes it. A
picture already on the device shows at once, no flash of the note.

**What did not change.** The verdict itself. *No online source* still
means what it meant: two minutes with nobody answering. The node keeps
asking on its own schedule after that, and the sentence comes down by
itself when somebody does.

**Measured on the stand.** An 8 MB video through the Moscow relay from
the catalog on the Amsterdam one: the note at once, the player in nine
seconds. A 240 KB image: five.
