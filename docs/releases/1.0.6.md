# 1.0.6 — solid ground under every message

A small release, the same day as 1.0.5, for one thing the owner's eye
caught on the first screen: in the dark theme, message bubbles were
grey glass — the sky showed through them. They are **solid and dark**
now, yours and everybody else's: incoming near-neutral graphite, your
own with the violet whisper it always had. A message sits on its own
ground.

Nothing else moved.
