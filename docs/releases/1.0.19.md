# 1.0.19 — a relay that just answered is not "cooling down"

**A network blip no longer costs minutes.** When the Mac's connection
hiccups — sleep, a Wi‑Fi change, a short outage — the node's pooled
connections to the relays die in silence, the next writes time out, and
the pool's breaker held that relay closed for up to two minutes as if
the relay had failed. Meanwhile the doorbell listener had already
re-parked on that very relay over a fresh handshake. Measured on the
owner's Mac: a message took 2.6 minutes to reach a relay that had been
answering for most of them, with "relay · issue" on screen.

Now an acknowledged park is taken as the proof it is: the breaker for
that relay lets go, its dead sockets are dropped, and the sender's lane
tries at once instead of after its own ladder.

**The cooling error names the relay.** "relay is cooling down after
failures — 195.63.160.237:7411", so a healthy primary is not the first
thing somebody checks.

No relay change.
