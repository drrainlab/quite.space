# 1.0.22 — the app no longer slides sideways, and a phone says why nothing arrives

**Android: the screen could be dragged sideways into emptiness, and twitched.**
That was 1.0.21's own doing: a decorative layer in the header drifted a few
pixels past the edge of the screen, which made the whole app a little wider
than the phone and let a thumb pull it. The layer is gone and the shell now
cannot scroll at all — only the things inside it do.

**"relay · issue" while everything plainly worked.** The light went red on
any failure anywhere in a sync cycle — a relay that belongs to somebody you
talk to, an official relay your VPN cannot reach, a followed space whose
publisher is away. With thirty spaces there is nearly always one. The light
is now about *your own* relay: if it answered, it stays healthy, and the
other endpoint's complaint is the chip's tooltip. Spaces that are really
waiting still say so on their own row.

**Notifications on Android — what was found, what changed.** There is no
push service, by design, so a dark phone has to look after itself. Three
faults are fixed: after Android closed the app, the "Stay connected" service
came back *without reopening the node*, so nothing synced until you opened
Quiet yourself; a start-up race could drop the first notification after a
crash; and re-arming ran on the interface thread. And **Settings → This
device** now opens with *Why nothing may arrive* — each line shown only
while it is true: Android is not allowing notifications (which switches
them off at the source) with a button to the system setting; battery
optimisation is on, which cuts a sleeping app's network, with a button
that raises Android's own "run unrestricted?" question; and the passphrase
is not remembered, so a closed app cannot reopen itself.
*Honesty note: these Android changes compile and pass their tests, but were
written without a phone on the desk. If notifications still do not reach
you after this update, that section tells you which of the three it is —
and if it shows nothing, please tell us.* Expected delay for a backgrounded
phone remains one to three minutes, or seconds with the doorbell
(UnifiedPush) switched on.

**The list of spaces can be made wider.** Drag its right edge (or focus it
and use the arrow keys; double-click to reset). The width is remembered on
this device.

**Smaller things.** On a wide screen the orbit stands alone on the left of
the header; the connection and the four actions sit on the right with
settings and your face. "public · read only" moved from the room's bar into
the space's panel, where the place's other facts live, so the name has room.
The header's glow became a few faint stars. Room colours are a little more
alive: each kind of space has two neighbouring hues, and send, invite and
your own bubbles run between them. Changing your name no longer fails when
you have kept a read-only public space.

No relay change. No protocol change.
