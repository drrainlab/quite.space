# 1.0.25 — the doorbell reaches a sleeping phone

**Android: messages arrive while the phone sleeps on a table.** 1.0.24
kept the node open in a pocket; a tester still received nothing after
locking his phone and everything the moment he opened the app. The cause
was not the node: within minutes of the screen going dark, Android cuts a
sleeping app's network and closes nothing, so our connection to the relay
looked alive and heard nothing — and every measurement behind 1.0.24 had
been taken on a charger, where that never happens.

Two things changed.

- **The relay no longer trusts a connection it cannot hear from.** When mail
  lands and the parked phone does not come to collect it within twelve
  seconds, the relay rings the out-of-band doorbell anyway. Before, a
  sleeping phone's dead socket silenced the doorbell for up to 45 minutes.
- **The doorbell has a carrier on ordinary phones.** The platform's own push
  lane is the one channel Android keeps open through sleep; on phones with
  Google services that is Firebase Cloud Messaging. The relay's contentless
  ping now goes to `push.quite.space`, which forwards a contentless,
  high-priority wake to the phone. On the owner's phone, off the battery
  whitelist and with "stay connected" off: the ring lands about two seconds
  after the message, the notification follows in one to ten seconds.

**What Google learns, said where the switch is:** that this installation
had something to check, and when. Not what, not from whom, not where — the
relay's ping is a fixed marker, the gateway forwards a fixed marker, and no
hint, sender, space or count exists anywhere on that path. It is on by
default and it is a switch: *Settings → This device → Doorbell*. A
UnifiedPush distributor (ntfy and friends), once turned on there, carries
the doorbell instead and tells Google nothing. Phones without Google
services keep the parked connection and the poll, as before.

**Smaller things in the same release.**
- The doorbell rings only for what is worth waking a phone for: your mail,
  a knock from a device pairing with you, a guest at one of your standing
  invites. Housekeeping between your own devices waits for the next poll.
- A platform wake starts by dropping the connections sleep left half-open,
  so the few seconds of network Android grants are not spent on a dead
  socket (the first build did exactly that, and the notification never came).
- The battery question — "let Quiet run unrestricted?" — is asked once per
  install, by the system's own dialog, after notifications are settled.
  It is what lets the parked connection live through sleep between rings.
- The gateway is open source in this repository (`cmd/quiet-push`), keeps
  nothing, and logs nothing about you.

Known and not done here: the relay keeps doorbell registrations in memory,
so a relay restart forgets a sleeping phone until it next wakes; and the
"delivered" mark still trails the other phone's notification by a sync cycle.

Relays: updated. Protocol: one added key on the park message, skipped by
older relays.
