# 1.0.10 — Quiet Signal, a voice of its own

```
   ·  ✦   ·        Quiet Signal · Тихий сигнал          ·   ✦
 ✦    ·     ·      small packets, wide horizons          ·      ·
```

The app has its own typeface now. **Quiet Signal** is a wide,
square-shouldered monoline grotesque with Latin and Cyrillic, drawn as
skeletons and grown into two weights by the build — the same letter at
a thinner stroke is the Regular, at a heavier one the Bold. It was
sketched against the owner's type studies, checked on a live stand and
corrected by their eye twice in one night: apertures opened, the wide
Cyrillic narrowed, the weights lightened, and one honest bug — every
o, O, 0 and 8 had shipped as a solid dot — caught and fixed before it
reached anyone.

**Where you will see it.** Where the app names things: a space's name in
the navigator, a panel's title. Not in the body of a message and not in
a post — a display face at reading sizes is a poster, not a paragraph,
and the reading face stays what it is.

**What it is not yet.** Kerned, hinted, or italic. A text weight for
paragraphs is a different typeface. The source — every letter as a few
strokes — lives in the repository under `tools/typeface`, under the SIL
Open Font License.
