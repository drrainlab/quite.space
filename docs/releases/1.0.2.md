# 1.0.2 — a familiar door, and a sky behind it

```
   ·  ✦   ·     Orbit · 2 people                       ·   ✦
 ✦    ·     ·   You: ✦ a shared sky        16:37       ·      ·
```

The first release in the 1.0 line without a suffix. Two waves, one
lesson: every field complaint of the past week was about *legibility*,
not features — "read or not?", "the history is unclear", "I don't see
the room". People arrive from ordinary messengers, and each unfamiliar
pixel is a tax on entry.

**🚪 The familiar shell.** The list on the left reads like an inbox
now: the room's mark, its name, the last moment with who said it, the
time. Under a room's name it says who is here — *2 people* — and the
kind of place it is moved to the hover. Two new views sit right after
the conversation in every room: **Files** (every photo, voice note,
track and document anyone shared) and **Links** (every link — a link
card or the first URL inside a message). Neither writes anything new;
both are the same log, read as a shelf. And every room gets a mark from
a built-in set of twenty line icons — the manifest carries only the
NAME, an unknown one falls back to the generated glyph, so old and new
clients agree on what they see.

What we took from the messengers is the layout. What we refused is
their smuggled promises: no "5 drawing now", no automatic *online*, no
typing lights, no calls. Presence remains what a person declares.

**✦ A sky.** A shared drawing that is a message
([ADR-036](../../adr/ADR-036-a-sky-is-strokes-not-a-canvas.md)).
There is no canvas object that everybody edits: a sky is one moment in
the chat, and every gesture is its own small signed event pointing at
it — points on a 128×128 grid, one byte each, three levels of glow.
Strokes commute, so the picture is the union and the log is the merge;
the film order is the same on every replica, which is what makes
*"watch how it was drawn"* possible. An erase removes only your own
strokes. The colour is the person — every client derives the hue from
your principal, so who drew what needs no legend. Nothing is sent while
the finger moves and nobody is told who is drawing; the picture updating
is the only presence. A sky cools at four thousand strokes and says so.

Start one from the attach menu (📎 → *sky*). Clients on earlier builds
see "✦ a shared sky" as a line of text and nothing breaks.

**Also in this build.** Delivery checkmarks now climb in place — the
✓✓ used to wait for another change on the row before repainting.
