# 1.0.1-rc.2 — delivered means acknowledged

```
   ·  ✦   ·      QI FRAME ──▶ stand ──▶ node        ·   ✦
 ✦    ·     ·    ◀── QI ACK <event id>  (or nothing)   ·      ·
```

A fix release, one day after rc.1, for what the first evening on a wall
wart taught us: "378 moments arrived ahead of what came before them and
wait for it".

**🔗 No more holes in an instrument's chain.** The USB stand used to be
trusted on attendance — a stand that spoke in the last 45 seconds counted
as present. Pull the cable or close the app and, for those 45 seconds,
the board printed readings into nobody, cleared them as delivered and
moved its chain on; everything the Wi-Fi courier carried afterwards
arrived behind a gap nothing could fill. Now the stand acknowledges every
frame it applied by event id, the board treats a frame as delivered only
when that acknowledgement arrives, and a frame the node refused is never
acknowledged — the board keeps owing it instead of skipping ahead.

**⏱ No minute-long stall after a reconnect.** The chain of roads used to
punish the Wi-Fi courier for not having dialed yet — three strikes in the
first 150 ms of every boot, a minute in exile. A road that is not ready
is now skipped, not struck; readings ride Wi-Fi the moment the door
answers.

**📓 The door keeps a diary.** Every knock the node answers or refuses is
one line in its own log, with the reason. The wire stays silent — that is
the door's law — but the person debugging their own board no longer has
to guess.

**One board carries one more clean re-enrollment.** A chain already holed
cannot be mended — the missing predecessor is gone, and a re-based chain
would read as a fork — so a board that met the old stand wipes once and
enrolls fresh. The panel hides the silent ghost by itself.

Verified on the owner's desk before this tag: plug in, connect, the
card appears with the first reading; unplug, and the readings keep
arriving over Wi-Fi with the held count at zero.
