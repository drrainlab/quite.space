# 1.0.24 — messages arrive in a pocket, with a name on them

**Android: Quiet keeps receiving while the phone is locked.** Android closes
apps — an update, a battery saver's sweep — and brings our background
service back in seconds. Until now that service could only reopen the node
on an *unlocked* phone, and not at all if your passphrase was sealed behind
a code or a face. 1.0.23 taught it to say "something is waiting"; it still
could not tell you what, or deliver anything, until you opened the app. For
somebody waiting on a word that matters, that is a messenger failing.

Now the node opens by itself in the background, locked screen or not, and
keeps receiving: messages arrive, delivery is confirmed to the sender, photos
and voice notes you hold are handed to the people asking for them. The
passphrase it opens with is kept under a second key in the phone's own
secure hardware — it never leaves the device, and a copied file or a backup
is useless — but said plainly: **with this on, a locked phone is no longer a
second wall in front of Quiet.** Somebody who defeats Android's own lock
finds the node open. Every other messenger on your phone makes this trade;
it is on by default and it is a switch: *Settings → This device → While the
phone is locked → Wait for unlock* brings back the old rule, and then Quiet
opens the moment you unlock the phone rather than when you find its icon.
After a reboot nothing opens before your first unlock either way — Android
keeps every app's files sealed until then.

**Your code and your face now lock the screen — every time.** They used to
guard the *opening of the node*, so they were asked only when the node was
closed; with "stay connected" on, that was almost never, and the app opened
without a question for as long as it had been running. Now they are asked
whenever you come back after a minute away, whether or not the node is open:
"Locked. Quiet is still receiving." The interface is not drawn until you
answer.

- Fixed along the way: ten wrong codes erased the code — and the *next
  launch opened with no question at all*, because a remembered passphrase was
  still lying next to it. The remembered copy now goes with the code, and
  only your passphrase opens the app after that.

**Notifications say who wrote.** A new mode, "Who wrote", and the default
for anybody who has not chosen: the space and the sender, always; the words
only while the phone is unlocked — a locked screen shows "New message". When
you unlock, the card quietly fills in; when the screen locks again, the words
leave. We enforce this ourselves rather than rely on Android's own lock-screen
redaction, which ships switched off on most phones. If you chose a mode
before, it stays as you chose it — "Nothing" still tells Android nothing at
all. (This setting also used to snap back to its default every time the app
restarted on Android; it is remembered now.)

**A ring from the relay fetches your mail first.** Measured on a locked
phone that reads a dozen public spaces: 26 seconds from the relay's ring to
the notification. The ring only woke the full sync cycle, and that cycle
collects your own mail *last* — after every public space. A ring now
collects your mail on a lane of its own, which the cycle cannot occupy. On
the same phone: one to three seconds.

**A guest is let in while your phone is dark.** You share a link, lock the
phone, and your guest sat at "waiting for the owner" until you happened to
open the app — nobody was listening at the invite's own mailbox, only
polling it, every three minutes in the background. The relay rings for it
now, like for any other mailbox.

Relays: no change needed. No protocol change.

Not in this release, and known: the "delivered" mark can still trail the
