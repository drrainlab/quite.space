# 1.0.8 — the sky opens on its own

The owner's eye, an hour after 1.0.7: a post with an atmosphere opened
on a still picture and two doors, and the sky nobody opened was a sky
nobody saw. That rule — *still and silent until a door is used* — was
written when the floor under the scenes was new. The floor is not new
any more, and it is what makes an unasked picture safe.

**Opening the post opens its picture.** Within whatever you set for
atmospheres (off, poster, calm, full), whatever the space permits and
whatever your system's *reduce motion* says — the stage decides, as it
always did. The bar reads *moving* from the first frame, with **Pause**
and **Leave**.

**Sound still asks.** It is the one thing that does: **With sound** is
the only door left, and it reads as a door, not as a toggle that
implies the sound was ever on. A remembered yes, recorded at your own
request, still applies to held spaces and never to a transient preview.

**The feed is unchanged.** A card is still a marker and a still frame;
nothing moves until you open the post. Twenty live canvases is a feed
nobody can read.
