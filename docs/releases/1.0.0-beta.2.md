# 1.0.0-beta.2 — where you actually went

```
   ·  ✦   ·      ── ─── ●───●──●╌╌╌╌●──●─ ───       ·   ✦
 ✦    ·     ·        no fix · 52 s ↑              ·      ·
```

The map already answered "where are you now?" — positions as signed,
expiring claims that age honestly in front of you. This release answers
the other question a search operation asks: **"where did you actually go
during this operation?"** They are different questions, and they stay
different mechanisms.

**🥾 Field Sessions.** On a sector or a place: *Start sweep*. The phone
keeps recording with the screen off, in a pocket, for as long as the
operation runs — and every clause of how that is allowed to happen is
load-bearing. Background location exists **only** inside an explicitly
started, visibly active, bounded session: it begins with your press on a
button that says what it does, a persistent notification names the
operation for its whole duration and carries the Stop that ends it, and
the manifest still declares no background-location permission at all —
recording with no session and no visible card is structurally
impossible, not merely against policy. It is all in
[ADR-034](../../adr/ADR-034-a-sweep-is-a-bounded-session.md).

**A gap is a sample, not a silence.** GPS was absent for 52 seconds; the
phone slept; two fixes merely happened far apart — those are different
claims about the world, and a bare polyline renders them identically.
The track format makes every gap an item a reader must consume, with a
cause the recorder can actually defend: `no_fix` only when the platform
itself said so, `suspended` only when sleep was measured by the clock
pair that keeps ticking through it, `unknown` otherwise — a recorder
that does not know says so, it never invents. The map draws a gap as a
gap; the GPX export breaks its segments in the same places. The system
never draws a line through a forest because it wanted a continuous one.

**Kill the app mid-sweep and the recording survives** — it lives in the
node, not the screen. The restarted service asks the node whether the
session still runs and claims it back, wearing the seam as one honest
suspended gap. A sweep nobody comes back for finalizes as *interrupted*
with the walked track preserved — and its task stays open, because a ✓
on work that was not finished would be a lie.

**When it ends, the fact travels first.** `sweep.completed.v1` is a
radio-sized event — 348 bytes worst-case against the 500-byte LoRa
frame, measured cold — so a receiver two ridges away learns *"Sector B3
swept · 13:02–13:47 · 2.7 km · nothing found"* the moment it happens.
The detailed track is an asset and arrives when broadband does. LoRa:
what happened. Broadband: the full how.

**🖐 A place is born from a finger on the map.** Arm it, tap where it
is, name it. Typed coordinates were never offered on purpose — a number
in a form is a different claim than a finger on the map — but until now
the only door was the API. The flow lands you on the new place's card,
where the sweep button is.

**📎 An observation can point at its evidence.** "Нашёл ботинок, вот
фото" — one photo per observation, deliberately: an observation is a
sentence plus one piece of evidence, and three photos are three
observations. The note stays radio-sized (the photo travels as media,
the event carries a 32-byte reference), so the fact crosses the radio
now and the picture follows when a fatter pipe appears.

---

Measured, not assumed: two live sessions on open water — 29 minutes and
an hour and a half, screen off, no cable — recorded 472 points at the
designed 15-second cadence with zero gaps and no wake lock held. That
number is why v1 holds no wake lock: the decision stands on evidence,
and on hardware where Doze does bite, the bite becomes honest
`suspended` gaps rather than a corrupted track.

Two formats join the frozen `.v1` families: `field.track.v1` and
`sweep.completed.v1`. Everything ADR-033 froze in beta.1 stays frozen;
everything it left free stays free. The beta suffix still marks the
reach of our evidence, nothing else.
