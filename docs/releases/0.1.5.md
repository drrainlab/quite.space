# 0.1.5 — one identity, many devices — and media that arrives

Two promises this release makes good on, and neither is small.

**🧍 You are one person on all your devices.** Join a space on your
phone — it appears on your paired laptop, by itself, even if the laptop
was asleep in a bag when it happened. Add someone, get added, receive a
key rotation: every device you certified converges on the same
relationships and the same access, whichever device did the acting.
Pairing no longer copies a snapshot; it introduces a new device into an
existing you. And revoking a device means exactly what it should: no
new access from that point on — nothing it lawfully held is pretended
away, and your other devices carry on.

**📦 Media arrives.** The beta's sorest wound — the photo that never
loads, the video stuck at zero — came down to routes: delivery
guessed where your devices listen, wrote the guess down as knowledge,
and then trusted it forever. All of that is replaced.

- **Photos, voice notes and small files now travel WITH the message.**
  The moment you press send — the one moment your phone is certainly
  awake — the bytes ride ahead into each recipient's mailbox. Opening
  the card finds them already home. Your phone can go back into the
  pocket; it is no longer needed.
- **Big videos stream.** Playback starts from local bytes decrypted
  chunk by chunk — seeking is instant, and a 380 MB file no longer
  freezes the player that just downloaded it.
- **Devices say where they live.** Every message you send quietly
  carries where your device can be reached, and the newest statement
  wins — so a device that moved to another relay, or joined before it
  had an address at all, becomes reachable the first time it speaks.
- **Waiting is said out loud.** "Reaching the sender…" is a stage, not
  a frozen bar; a holder that cannot answer records why, in the
  transport's own words, and the diagnostics screen now carries the
  verdicts — copyable on macOS too, at last.

**🌊 And media got a visual language of its own.** Not upload and
download — release and arrival. A picture sharpens as its bytes land: half
the file, half the softness. A waveform materialises bar by bar. Your
own freshly sent media keeps a faintly living surface until the
hand-over is confirmed, then stills. Movement means something is
happening; stillness means the object has landed. No spinners anywhere.

Smaller honesties that rode along:

- The interface stopped re-downloading the whole room every two seconds
  over the app's internal bridge — your battery noticed.
- The send dialog answers the press: buttons rest while a large video
  is being sealed, so an impatient second tap cannot post it twice.
- The video player stays inside its card on a phone.
- A quick link you already opened answers from memory instead of
  scaring you with "nothing waiting" over a join that was going fine.

**Update both sides.** The device that HOLDS the bytes must be on 0.1.5
for its answers to find you — one old phone in the pair keeps the old
silence. The official relays are already updated to match.
