# 1.0.1-rc.1 — the board found the door by itself

```
   ·  ✦   ·      ┌─ Heltec ─┐  ~ wifi ~  ┌─ your node ─┐      ·   ✦
 ✦    ·     ·    │ 23.1 °C  │ ─────────▶ │  ✦ Мастерская │  ·      ·
```

A sensor on a shelf, a laptop in another room, and no cable between them.
This release is the second episode of Quiet Instruments: the board
carries its own readings over the local network, and the app grows the
things a person needs to live with a physical instrument — plug it in
with one click, see when something new arrived, end a ghost.

**📡 Wi-Fi courier (QI-B1).** A provisioned board joins your network, hears
your node announce itself, recognises its own space from the hint, dials
over TLS and knocks with a signature bound to your node's certificate.
Readings travel up that connection; key rotations and the clock travel
down it. USB becomes the rescue road, not the only one: unplug it and the
readings keep arriving. The board takes its time from the network (NTP),
so a power cut in the hall does not leave it deaf. Laws and the live gate
are in [ADR-035](../../adr/ADR-035-a-bearer-is-a-courier-not-a-member.md).

**🔌 Plug in, click.** The USB stand moved into the app. Open a space,
plug a board in, press *Connect USB instrument* — the node enrolls it,
provisions it, and remembers the choice across restarts. *Disconnect*
stops the stand and nothing else.

**🆔 Protocol view shows the space id**, with a copy button — what a stand,
a script or another node needs to name a space.

**✦ Something new, said quietly.** A space with moments you have not
seen since you looked earns one small breathing star beside its name and
a faint halo on its glyph. Opening it quiets the star. No badges, no
counts, no red.

**🧊 Relief.** The verbs that carry intent — make a space, enter,
discover, signals, invite, the view's own act — tilt faintly toward you,
throw a shadow back and breathe. Kept things, posts and objects wear a
spine and lean toward the cursor. Motion respects your reduced-motion
setting.

**👻 A ghost can be ended.** An instrument that stopped speaking — an
identity a board minted and abandoned — used to leave a card forever,
and "remove" answered *unknown instrument*. An instrument is a member
while it speaks under the current epoch: cards appear with the first
reading and leave with the key, on every replica at once.

**🕳️ Held, not lost.** The space panel now says when moments arrived
ahead of what came before them and are waiting: signed, verified, and
parked. It is the line that found this release's deepest bug.

**What the board taught us.** Its first reboot on a desk exposed that a
board never restored its identity (every reboot minted a new one); its
first night on a wall wart, that the USB stand claimed delivery to a
cable nobody was reading. Both are fixed here and pinned by tests. The
release notes say this so nobody has to rediscover it.

**What is still not true.** A real board publishes into *sealed* spaces
only — plaintext telemetry into a broadcast space is the next episode.
The Wi-Fi courier needs 2.4 GHz and keeps the radio awake (~40 mA); a
network that filters multicast can name the node statically. Discovery
on the Android host still pauses with the screen — a phone in a pocket
is not an instrument's node.
