# 1.0.5 — a place where it is clear what is happening and what you can do

```
   ·  ✦   ·     Invite → into this space · a new conversation      ·   ✦
 ✦    ·     ·   then: link · QR · sound                            ·      ·
```

An outside review of the shipped screens said it in one sentence: *the
interface explained internal mechanics where it need not, and failed
to explain consequences where it must.* We agreed, wrote down three
scenarios a newcomer should manage with no explanation — invite a
friend, handle a delayed message, read a sensor's freshness — and
closed three waves against them.

**The shell says less, and says it plainly.** The space panel reads in
the order a person asks: **Now** (instruments, radio — only when
something is live) → People → About this place → Manage. A stale
reading says *no fresh data · last reading 6 days ago* before it says a
number. The header's four ways in share one quiet hue, and the first
says what it does: *create*. Words for the wait: *saved on this device —
sent when a path opens*; a presence age carries its label. A link in a
conversation is a row until you open it.

**Navigation by meaning.** Where you go to find what was published is
one place now — **Materials**: posts, files, links, filters inside. The
shelf and objects each keep their tab with one sentence about what it
is for, where a newcomer meets it.

**Invitations by intent.** Inviting used to begin with a protocol
entity. It begins with a decision now: **into this space** (a pass —
they ask to enter, this device lets them in) or **a new conversation**
(five words or a link that open a new place); the carrier — link, QR,
sound, words — comes after. Entering is one field: paste a link, a
pass, or type the words you heard; it tells them apart. Previewing an
invitation never spends it.

**Profile & devices.** One overview, and each task opens from it: start
a conversation, verify this device, backup — its state first: *no
backup yet* or *last backup N days ago* — technical details, rename.

Nothing in the protocol moved. Presence remains what a person declares;
there is still no "online", no typing light, no read receipt.
