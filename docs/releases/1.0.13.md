# 1.0.13 — the picture is the control

**Loading media: one tap on the thing itself.** The way to load a photo
used to be a text link under it — "⬇ fetch original · 1.2 MB · not local
yet" — eleven-point type between two pictures. Gone. Tap the picture,
the poster, the waveform or the file card, and the same surface answers
with a small glowing ring: turning while the relay is asked, breathing
and dimmer while a sender is looked for, filling as the bytes land, gone
when they are here. The message itself resonates faintly meanwhile. No
spinner and no separate status line — the ring is the status, and the
numbers stay under the card as the detail.

A tap is kept as a wish: a photo tapped before it was here opens the
moment it is; a sound or a video tapped plays when it lands.

**A late answer is still an answer.** A holder that replied after the
fetch had given up (two minutes of silence; a phone in the background
answers on its own cadence) used to leave the projection saying "did
not answer" while the chunks were visibly landing. Now the first chunk
clears the verdict and the fetch resumes for the rest.

Also in this line since 1.0.11: "I moved" — a device that changes relays
tells its peers at once, and a live device with no known route is
guessed at every official relay (1.0.12).
