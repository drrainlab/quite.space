LAN peer

nodes on one network usually find each other by multicast announce. If discovery is filtered (common on many networks, and between two nodes on one machine), connect directly: the peer's port is shown in their header as LAN :PORT.

meshtastic radio

connect a node: tcp:HOST[:PORT] (WiFi node / meshtasticd) or serial:/dev/tty… (USB). Sync over LoRa is slow by physics — summaries go out once a minute.

blind relay

a relay holds encrypted bundles for peers who are offline. It sees a rotating hint and ciphertext — nothing else. Its receipt means accepted by relay, never “delivered”.
